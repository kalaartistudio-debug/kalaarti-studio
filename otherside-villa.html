Otherside Weekend Villa

Location
Gandhinagar
Area
3100 sq.ft.
Type
Residential
Status
Completed

This weekend villa is designed as a serene contemporary retreat inspired by the moon and its quiet luminosity. A double-height living space with a suspended swing forms the spatial core, enhanced by natural light, soft neutral tones, and subtle blue accents.

Clean lines, layered wall paneling, and controlled material transitions create a calm, cohesive environment rooted in both concept and comfort. Every space is thoughtfully designed to facilitate relaxation and connection with nature.

The design embraces minimalism while maintaining warmth and livability, creating the perfect sanctuary for weekend escapes from the city.

Key Spaces

Entrance Area

Serene introduction to the villa. The entrance sets the tone for the entire space, welcoming guests with a sense of calm and anticipation. Every element from the threshold onwards has been carefully considered to evoke the tranquility that defines this weekend retreat.

Double Height Living

Spatial core with suspended swing. The iconic double-height living space with its suspended swing forms the heart of the villa. This spatial drama, combined with carefully orchestrated natural light, creates an atmosphere of openness and wonder that invites relaxation and contemplation.

Interior Details

Moon-inspired aesthetic elements. Soft neutral tones of whites, creams, and soft greys form the foundation, while subtle blue accents echo the night sky. Every material selection, from wall finishes to furnishings, reinforces the serene, moonlit atmosphere.

Design Concept

Interior Spaces

Serene and contemporary design throughout. The villa embraces minimalism while maintaining warmth and livability. Each interior space feels like a quiet sanctuary, designed for those who seek respite from the everyday.

Design Elements

Layered paneling and materials. Clean lines, layered wall paneling, and controlled material transitions create a calm, cohesive environment. Every design decision is rooted in both concept and comfort, supporting the villa's overall moon-inspired narrative.

Lighting Design

Natural light integration with intentional placement. Carefully positioned openings flood the villa with natural light during the day, while thoughtfully designed artificial lighting creates a luminous atmosphere at night, echoing the gentle glow of the moon.

A Serene Escape

The Otherside Weekend Villa represents the perfect balance between architectural sophistication and comfortable living. It's a space designed for those who seek respite from the everyday, where every detail contributes to a sense of peace and wonder.

From the moon-inspired color palette to the iconic suspended swing, every element tells a story of thoughtful design and deep understanding of what makes a space truly special.
