One World 3BHK Apartment

Location
Rajkot, Gujarat
Area
1400 sq.ft.
Type
Residential
Status
Completed

The residence follows a modern Indian design approach with a cohesive neutral palette of beige, brown, warm grey, and soft white, accented with selective bright colours to add vibrancy and character.

The living area features a black marble–finish TV unit with veneer and black painted glass accents. The dining space is defined by olive green tones, soft white leather seating, and a veneer-framed mirror wall artifact.

The master bedroom is designed in beige and brown hues with tropical wallpaper and bronze mirror detailing, creating a calm yet refined private retreat.

Living Spaces

Living Area

Contemporary living with black marble TV unit. The living area features a striking black marble-finish TV unit with veneer and black painted glass accents, creating a modern focal point that anchors the space while maintaining elegant simplicity.

Dining Area

Olive green accents with elegant furniture. The dining space is defined by rich olive green tones, soft white leather seating, and a veneer-framed mirror wall artifact that adds depth and sophistication to the gathering space.

Design Palette

Modern Indian design approach with a cohesive neutral palette of beige, brown, warm grey, and soft white, accented with selective bright colours. This thoughtful combination creates a harmonious environment that blends traditional warmth with contemporary style.

Bedrooms

Master Bedroom

The master bedroom is designed as a calm and private retreat, focusing on comfort, warmth, and visual continuity. A neutral palette of beige, brown, and soft grey creates a soothing base, supporting a relaxed and balanced atmosphere.

A layered false ceiling with recessed profiles and warm cove lighting enhances spatial depth and creates an intimate, restful mood. The bed-back wall is finished with tropical wallpaper and bronze mirror detailing.

Kid's Bedroom

The kid's bedroom is designed to be calm, cheerful, and functional. A neutral base of white and beige keeps the space light, while mustard yellow accents add warmth and a playful character.

A soft board is integrated with the cushioned bed headboard and study table, providing a cohesive surface for notes and artwork. Wooden finishes introduce a natural, grounding feel to the room.

Design Details

Thoughtful spatial planning ensures every room serves multiple purposes while maintaining aesthetic harmony. From storage solutions to flexible furniture arrangements, the design prioritizes both form and function.

Design Highlights

This residence exemplifies modern Indian design sensibilities, where traditional warmth meets contemporary sophistication. The careful selection of materials, colors, and spatial organization creates a home that is both aesthetically refined and wonderfully livable.

Each room tells a story of comfort and elegance, from the minimalist kitchen to the jewel-toned dining area, and the serene bedrooms that offer peaceful retreats.
